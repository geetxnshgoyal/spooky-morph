Test Morph on Android

Morph is in closed testing on Google Play and opens to everyone around 11 November 2026. Testers get it first, and help it launch: Google needs testers to keep it installed for 14 days before it can go public. It takes three steps and about two minutes.

  1. Join the testers group

    Open the group and click Join group. Use the same Google account as the Play Store on your phone. Joining is what gives your account access; your email is only visible to me.

    Join Morph testers

  2. Become a tester

    Open this link signed in with the same Google account and tap Become a tester. If it says the app isn't available, wait a few minutes after joining the group and try again.

    Become a tester

  3. Install from Google Play

    Morph's Play Store page now opens for you. Install it like any other app; updates arrive through the Play Store too. Android 10 or later. If the page says the app isn't available, check that the Play Store on your phone uses the account that joined the group.

    Get it on Google Play

What to try

  • Convert a photo to JPG, PNG or WEBP, or turn an SVG into a PNG.
  • Compress a photo with a size limit: Settings → Size limit → 200 KB.
  • Share a file to Morph from your gallery or file manager.
  • Turn a PDF into images, or trace a logo into an SVG.

Please stay for 14 days

Keep Morph installed and stay in the group for at least 14 days. Leaving early resets Google's count, and the public launch waits for it.

Found a problem?

Send private feedback from Morph's page in the Play Store, or open an issue on GitHub with your phone model and what you did. Everything you try stays on your phone; Morph has no internet access at all.

Don't want to wait? The APK is on the GitHub releases page, but installing it that way doesn't count as a Play tester.
