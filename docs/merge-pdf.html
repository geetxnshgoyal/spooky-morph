Merge PDFs without uploading them

Select two or more PDFs and Morph joins them into one, in the order you picked. Split works the other way round, one PDF per page.

How to do it

  1. Select the PDFs

    Two or more, in the order you want them.

  2. Choose Merge PDFs

    It's on the Tools wheel, or under Tools in the app on iPhone and iPad.

  3. Done

    The merged PDF is saved next to the first file as Merged.pdf.

Where it works

  • Mac Merge and split.
  • Windows Merge and split.
  • iPhone and iPad Merge and split, and Merge PDFs in Shortcuts.
  • Android Not yet. Android can turn images into a PDF and PDF pages into images.

Free and open source. Everything runs on your device: no uploads, no account, no ads, and your originals are never changed.

Questions

Is there a limit on pages or file size?

No limit is set by Morph; it's only bound by your device's memory.

Are my PDFs uploaded?

No. Merging happens on your device.
