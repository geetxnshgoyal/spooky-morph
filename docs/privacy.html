Morph

Privacy Policy

Morph collects nothing. Your files are converted on your own device and never leave it.

Last updated 28 October 2026. This policy covers Morph for iPhone and iPad, Mac, Windows and Android, and this website.

What Morph collects

Nothing. Morph has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. The apps make no network requests at all, so there is nothing to send anywhere. The Android app does not even ask for internet access, and its recent-jobs list and settings are kept out of Google's cloud backup.

Your files

  • Every conversion, compression and edit runs on your device. Files are never uploaded.
  • Originals are never changed. Results are saved as new files: in Morph's folder in the Files app on iPhone and iPad, beside the original on the Mac and on Windows, and in Downloads › Morph on Android.
  • The Mac App Store version can only open files you give it. It saves results to Downloads until you allow it, once, to save next to your files; Morph remembers only that permission.
  • Morph keeps a list of recent jobs on your device so you can find and undo them. Deleting the app removes it.

Photos

On iPhone and iPad, Morph asks for permission to add to your photo library only when you choose to save a result to Photos. It never reads your library; photos you pick to convert are handed to Morph by the system picker one at a time.

On Android, photos you pick come from the system photo picker, so Morph never asks for access to your photos or storage. Results are added to Downloads › Morph.

Settings

Your preferences, such as the compression level and whether haptics are on, are stored on your device and are not shared.

This website

This site has no analytics, no cookies and no tracking. Its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser fetches them from Google, which sees your IP address as part of that request.

Children

Morph collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Morph will not start collecting data without saying so here first.

Contact

Questions about privacy are welcome on the support page.
