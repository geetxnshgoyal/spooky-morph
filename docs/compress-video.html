Compress a video to send it

Phone videos are huge. Morph re-encodes them at a sensible size so they fit email and upload limits, with no watermark and no length cap.

How to do it

  1. Open the video in Morph

  2. Choose Compress

    Or set a size limit first, and Morph aims the video under it.

  3. Send it

    The smaller video is saved next to the original.

Where it works

  • Mac Yes, and size limits are most precise with ffmpeg installed (download from this site).
  • Windows Yes.
  • iPhone and iPad Yes, stepping down through export sizes to fit a limit.
  • Android Yes, choosing a bitrate from the video's length to fit a limit.

Free and open source. Everything runs on your device: no uploads, no account, no ads, and your originals are never changed.

Questions

How small can it get?

That depends on the length. A one-minute clip fits a few megabytes easily; an hour-long video won't fit 25 MB without looking rough.

Is there a watermark?

No. Morph is free and open source, with no watermark and no account.
