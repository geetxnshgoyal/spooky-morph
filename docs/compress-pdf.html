Compress a PDF

Scanned PDFs and documents full of photos get huge. Morph re-saves the images inside at screen resolution, so the file shrinks a lot while text stays sharp and selectable.

How to do it

  1. Open the PDF in Morph

    Drag it and tap ⌘ (Ctrl on Windows) for the Tools wheel, or share it to Morph on iPhone and iPad.

  2. Choose Compress

    Morph shows the new size, like 8.4 MB → 1.2 MB.

  3. Send it

    The smaller PDF is saved next to the original, which stays untouched.

Where it works

  • Mac Yes.
  • Windows Yes.
  • iPhone and iPad Yes, and in Shortcuts with Compress Files.
  • Android Not yet. Android compresses photos and videos.

Free and open source. Everything runs on your device: no uploads, no account, no ads, and your originals are never changed.

Questions

Will the text still be selectable?

Yes. Morph only re-encodes the images inside the PDF; text and vector drawings are kept as they are.

Why didn't my PDF get smaller?

PDFs that are mostly text are already small. Morph says so instead of making a bigger file.

Is it safe for confidential documents?

Yes. Nothing is uploaded; the PDF is processed on your device.
