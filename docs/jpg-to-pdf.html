JPG to PDF: turn photos into one PDF

Receipts, ID scans, homework pages: pick the photos and Morph puts them into a single PDF, one photo per page.

How to do it

  1. Select the photos

    JPG, PNG, HEIC, WEBP and more all work, and you can mix them.

  2. Choose PDF

    Drag them and tap Shift on a computer, or share them to Morph on your phone, then pick PDF.

  3. Share the PDF

    It's saved next to the first photo, ready to email or upload.

Where it works

  • Mac Yes.
  • Windows Yes.
  • iPhone and iPad Yes, straight from the Photos picker.
  • Android Yes, straight from the photo picker.

Free and open source. Everything runs on your device: no uploads, no account, no ads, and your originals are never changed.

Questions

What order do the pages come in?

The order you selected the photos in.

Can I make the PDF smaller?

Yes. On a Mac, Windows or iPhone, use Compress on the finished PDF, or set a size limit before converting the photos.

Does it need internet?

No. Morph works fully offline.
