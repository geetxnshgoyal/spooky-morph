Convert video to GIF

Screen recordings and short clips become GIFs that play anywhere, from chat apps to bug reports.

How to do it

  1. Open the clip in Morph

    Short clips make the best GIFs.

  2. Choose GIF

    It's on the Convert wheel with the video formats.

  3. Share it

    The GIF is saved next to the video.

Where it works

  • Mac Yes.
  • Windows Yes.
  • iPhone and iPad Yes.
  • Android Not yet.

Free and open source. Everything runs on your device: no uploads, no account, no ads, and your originals are never changed.

Questions

Why is my GIF large?

GIF is an old format that doesn't compress video well. Keep clips to a few seconds for small files.
