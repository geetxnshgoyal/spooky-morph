Resize an image to exact pixels

Need exactly 600 × 600, 1080 × 1080 or 1920 × 1080? Morph crops the image to fill that size, so there are no stretched faces and no white bars, and it can keep the file under a size limit too.

How to do it

  1. Set the photo size

    In Morph's Settings, under Photo size, pick a preset (square post 1080 × 1080, story 1080 × 1920, Full HD, profile picture 400 × 400 and more) or type any width × height.

  2. Open the images and choose Resize

    One image or a batch; each comes out at exactly that size.

  3. Done

    The resized JPG lands next to the original, named with the size, like photo 1080x1080.jpg.

Questions

Will it stretch my photo?

No. Morph keeps the proportions and trims the edges to fit, rather than squashing the picture.

Can I just make an image smaller without cropping?

Yes: set Photo size to Off and Resize fits the longest side to 2048 pixels, keeping the whole picture.
