Convert MOV to MP4

iPhone and Mac videos are often MOV, which some apps and websites refuse. Morph converts them to MP4 on your device, with no watermark and no size cap.

How to do it

  1. Open the video in Morph

    Drag and tap Shift on a computer, or share it to Morph on your phone.

  2. Choose MP4

    Or Compress, to make the video small enough to email or upload.

  3. Find it beside the original

    The MP4 keeps the original's name.

Where it works

  • Mac MP4 and MOV built in, plus WEBM and MKV with ffmpeg in the download from this site.
  • Windows MP4 built in, plus MOV, WEBM and MKV with ffmpeg.
  • iPhone and iPad MP4 and MOV, and Compress.
  • Android MP4, and Compress.

Free and open source. Everything runs on your device: no uploads, no account, no ads, and your originals are never changed.

Questions

Is there a watermark or a length limit?

No watermark, no limit, no account. Morph is free and open source.

Can I make a video smaller?

Yes. Use Compress, or set a size limit and Morph aims the video under it.
