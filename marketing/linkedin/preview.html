Building Morph
Now · 🌐
I got tired of uploading my files to random converter websites. So I built my own. 🌀 You know the drill: a HEIC photo that won't open, a form that wants your photo under 200 KB, a video too big to email. You search "converter", upload the file to a site you've never heard of, wait, download it, and hope they didn't keep a copy. Morph does all of that on your own device. On the Mac, you don't even open an app. Start dragging any file, tap ⇧, and a wheel of actions opens right under your cursor. Drop it on JPG, PDF, Compress or Remove Metadata, and the result lands next to the original. What it can do: → Convert images, video, audio, PDFs, documents, subtitles and CSV/JSON → Fit a photo under any upload limit: 200 KB for the exam form, 50 KB for the job portal → Strip location data, blur faces, and hide phone and card numbers in screenshots → Pull text out of images, read QR codes, merge and split PDFs It runs on Mac, iPhone, iPad and Android. On iPhone, every action also works in Shortcuts, so it plugs into Siri and the Action button. The part I care about most: nothing is ever uploaded. The Android app doesn't even ask for internet access. It's free and open source.
